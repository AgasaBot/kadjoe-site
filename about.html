Who we are

The builder designers
trust with their name

Established in 2017, Kadjoe stands at the meeting point of design and construction. Our team was founded by experts in building and interior who believe a great space is a balanced composition — the client's needs, honest function, and beauty that lasts.

That belief shows up in how we work: patient shop drawings, materials chosen by hand, engineering resolved before it's needed, and site teams who treat a hairline shadow-gap as a matter of honour.

It's why Indonesia's most exacting design studios — and the brands and families they serve — keep coming back.

Questions, answered

Before we begin

How long does a project take? +
It depends on scope and complexity. A residential interior typically runs from a few months to a year; larger commercial projects can extend further. We begin with a detailed consultation and give you an honest programme before work starts.
Do you work with our appointed designer? +
Gladly — most of our portfolio is built shoulder-to-shoulder with independent design studios, and we credit them on every project. We speak fluent designer: shop drawings, mock-ups, samples and site reviews.
Can Kadjoe design as well as build? +
Yes. Our in-house studio offers full design & build — one team, one contract, from concept to handover. See the in-house projects on our Design Partners page.
Where do you work? +
Everywhere the work is. We're based in West Jakarta and have delivered projects in sixteen cities — from Medan and Batam to Bandung, Semarang, Surabaya, Bali and Lombok.
