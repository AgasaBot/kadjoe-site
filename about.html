Who We Are

The builder designers
trust with their name

Kadjoe stands where design meets construction. Founded by experts in building and interior, we hold one belief: a great space is a balanced composition — the client's needs, honest function, and a beauty that outlasts the trend cycle.

The belief shows in the method. Patient shop drawings before a single panel is cut. Materials chosen by hand at the yard, not from a catalogue. Engineering resolved before it is needed, and site teams who treat a hairline shadow-gap as a matter of honour.

It is why Indonesia's most exacting design studios — and the families and brands they serve — keep returning.

Questions, Answered

Before we begin

How long does a project take? +
Honestly: it depends on scope. A residential interior typically runs from a few months to a year; larger commercial programmes longer. We begin with a detailed consultation, give you an honest programme before work starts — and keep to it.
Do you work with our appointed designer? +
Gladly — most of this portfolio was built shoulder-to-shoulder with independent studios, credited on every page. We speak fluent designer: shop drawings, mock-ups, samples and site reviews.
Can Kadjoe design as well as build? +
Yes. Kadjoe Studio offers full design-and-build — one team, one contract, from blank page to handover. Its work is marked “designed in-house” throughout the portfolio.
Where do you work? +
Wherever the work is. Based in West Jakarta; delivered in sixteen cities — Medan to Bali, Batam to Lombok.
