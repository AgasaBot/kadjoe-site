01 — The Studio

Great rooms are drawn by designers.
They are made by builders.

Kadjoe is the maker. We take the drawings interior designers pour themselves into — and the visions our own studio conceives — and build them to the millimetre: joinery, stone, steel, light, and the thousand quiet decisions in between.

From intimate homes to flagship restaurants and boutiques, every project is a balance of practicality and visual grace, finished to a standard our partners stake their names on.
